# 80B v2 production launch: SophiaG, constant-LR, scale brackets 512N/1024N/2048N

- **Date:** 2026-06-28 (submitted); launch attempted 2026-07-01 post-maintenance
- **Machine:** Aurora
- **Status:** 2048N CRASHED at init; 512N + 1024N running (2026-07-01). All 6
  jobs sat queued through the Mon 2026-06-29 maintenance; no head ran pre-PM.
  Post-PM the **2048N head (8574387) started first but SIGSEGV'd in
  `set_determinism`** at 24,864 ranks (`F`, Exit_status=143, 14:47 walltime) --
  the documented init-crash class (previously seen 2B/20B at 1024N), now
  confirmed at 2048N for 80B. Its continuation (8574390) was `qhold`'d (would
  repeat the identical deterministic crash). **512N (8574385, proven-safe) and
  1024N (8574386, the untested 80B data point) are the live brackets.** See
  Launch log for the crash + an auto-retry misclassification bug.

## Goal

Start 80B v2 production training via `scripts/submit_agpt_80b_autoretry.sh`
(native auto-retry), with three deliberate changes from the script defaults,
driven by the 2026-06-27 production-batch LR-finder and a continued-pre-training
(CPT) plan:

1. **Optimizer = SophiaG, LR = 1e-6** (not the script's AdamW@1e-6, which is on
   the NaN cliff at GBS~6144). The user initially cited "LR=2.5e-6 from the
   finder" -- that is SophiaG's *minimum* (loss 12.60); we use the finder
   README's conservative SophiaG rec **1e-6** (min/2.5) for safety, since
   SophiaG's stable band is narrow (blow-up onset ~4.6e-6).
2. **Constant LR after warmup (no decay)** via `DECAY_RATIO=0` (new script knob,
   commit b895ed0ba) -> `--lr-scheduler.decay-ratio=0`. The base config defaults
   to WSD decay (decay_ratio=0.8); CPT past the 4.67T budget needs flat LR.
3. **Validation-loss tracking ON** (95/5 split, the blendcorpus default) at
   `VALIDATOR_FREQ=500`. The "validator CCL deadlock at 80B TP=4" was confirmed
   a phantom and fixed 2026-06-28 (origin/ezpz a16e79caa); validator confirmed
   working at TP=4/dp=12 (job 12469784).

## Pre-check (job 8574347, 4N debug-scaling)

Before committing the big allocations, a 4N pre-check with the exact knobs
(sophiag/1e-6/decay-ratio=0, warmup=5, 20 steps) confirmed: the `DECAY_RATIO=0`
flag reaches the trainer (`"decay_ratio": 0.0` in printed config, schedule logs
"warmup then CONSTANT ... CPT mode"), SophiaG instantiates at lr=1e-6, cold
blendcorpus build is clean, and training descends (12.96 -> 10.10 over 19
steps). NOTE: an early grad_norm wobble (steps 9-12, grad_norm 45-73, loss
ticked up) was a **GBS=12 artifact** (SophiaG's Hessian term is batch-sensitive;
it is calibrated at GBS=6144) plus the normal early-warmup transient -- it
recovered by step 14 (grad_norm 26 -> 14, loss resumed descending). Not
representative of the GBS~6144 production batch; not a config problem.

## Risks (and why they are acceptable)

- **1024N/2048N init crash:** the only documented v2 large-N attempt (1024N,
  2026-05-04) crashed in `set_determinism` at 12,288 ranks; 2048N is 24,552
  ranks. The 512-class bracket (proven scale) is the safety net; the three
  brackets share no state, so a large-N init crash does not affect the others.
- **dp_degree ~6138 at 2048N** trips the script's >186 NaN warning (expected;
  that warning targets the AdamW grad-path corner; SophiaG cleared dp_degree=192
  in the finder, but 6138 is genuinely untested).
- **SophiaG narrow band:** 1e-6 is min/2.5 (safe per README); watch grad_norm in
  the first ~50 steps at the production batch.
- **Validator at dp=6138** is untested (confirmed only to dp=12);
  `VALIDATOR_ENABLE=0` is the escape hatch if it misbehaves.
- **Constant LR is DELIBERATE (CPT)** -- do not "fix" it back to decay.

## Launch log

### 2026-07-01 -- post-maintenance start (2048N first out)

All 6 jobs stayed queued through the Mon 2026-06-29 06:00 UTC maintenance
reservation; none ran before the machine went down, so there is no pre-PM
trajectory to resume -- these are cold starts.

Post-PM the **2048N head (8574387) launched first**, not the smaller brackets.
Sequence:

- **run_count 1-4 (~14:47-15:00 UTC): exec-server rejects.** The job repeatedly
  flipped Q -> R -> Q with `comment = Not Running: PBS Error: Execution server
  rejected request` and never wrote a log. Key diagnostic finding: *other users'
  large (2000+N) jobs were running fleet-wide during this window*, so it was NOT
  a machine-wide "can't place big jobs" state -- it was specific to 8574387's
  attempts. Read as post-maintenance node-release flapping (nodes cycling
  free/offline as the reservation tears down), so the standing decision was to
  wait and let PBS keep retrying rather than qdel+resubmit (which would forfeit
  accrued large-queue priority).
- **run_count 5 (~15:00 UTC): cleared.** The 5th attempt placed cleanly on 2072
  nodes and began writing `agpt-80b-n2046-sophiag.o8574387`: all hosts
  enumerated, `NHOSTS=2072 / NGPU_PER_HOST=12 / NGPUS=24864`, then into the
  `yeet_env` venv broadcast (`.venv.tar.gz` 2.7G -> `/tmp/.venv` on 2072 nodes,
  tarball mode). Zero error signals
  (`set_determinism`/SIGSEGV/EOFError/CCL/reject) at this point.
- **side effect:** grabbing 2072 nodes drove the free pool to ~72, so 8574385
  (512N) and 8574386 (1024N) went to `Not enough free nodes available` -- they
  backfill only once 2048N settles.

Operational takeaway: after a maintenance reservation tears down, a large job
can eat several exec-server rejects before the node set stabilizes. Waiting
(not requeuing) is the right call as long as *peer* large jobs are placing --
that distinguishes "our placement is flapping" from "the machine can't take
large jobs yet." Requeue only if peers are also failing (machine-wide) or the
reject count climbs well past a handful with peers succeeding (job-specific
wedge).

### 2026-07-01 -- 2048N SIGSEGV in set_determinism (init-crash wall at 24,864 ranks)

After clearing the venv broadcast and `ezpz launch` startup, the 2048N job
**crashed in `set_determinism` init** and went `F` (Exit_status=143, walltime
00:14:47). This is the **documented init-crash class** -- previously seen for 2B
(std::bad_alloc) and 20B (SIGSEGV) at 1024N/12,288 ranks (jobs 8463182/8463183)
-- now confirmed for **80B at 2048N/24,864 ranks**. The crash signature (CCL/PMI
KVS variant):

```
CCL_ERROR internal_kvs_server.hpp: get: read/write error: Connection reset by peer
CCL_ERROR pmi_resizable_simple_internal: get_local_kvs_id: failed to get local kvs id
CCL_ERROR atl_mpi_comm.cpp: init_transport: pmi init failed
Fatal Python error: Segmentation fault
  torchtitan/distributed/utils.py:231 in set_determinism   <- the broadcast
  torchtitan/experiments/ezpz/trainer.py:218 in __init__
rank 13602 died from signal 11 (SIGSEGV)
```

The fault is in the `torch.distributed.broadcast` inside `set_determinism`
(`utils.py:231`), same call site as the 1024N crashes. `set_determinism` is
model-agnostic (a distributed broadcast of the seed), consistent with this being
a rank-count scaling limit, not an 80B-specific issue. The CCL KVS "Connection
reset by peer" is the same libccl KVS-timeout family seen elsewhere at high rank
count during collective init.

**Two findings:**

1. **Init-crash ceiling for 80B is between 512N and 2048N, exactly bracketed by
   the 1024N run now in flight.** 512N (dp=1530) is proven; 2048N (dp=6138)
   crashes. 1024N (8574386, dp=3066) is the missing measurement -- it will
   either survive (ceiling is >1024N, i.e. ~2048N specific) or crash (ceiling is
   <=1024N and 512N is the practical max for 80B on this stack). Watch it.

2. **Auto-retry misclassified the SIGSEGV as a walltime stop.** The rank SIGSEGV
   tore down the launch via SIGTERM -> job `rc=143`, and
   `launch_autoretry.py:726` logged `[auto-retry] FAILOVER STOP: walltime
   (rc=143, attempt 1)` -- so it did NOT consume either of its 2 configured
   retries. Here it is *moot* (a deterministic init crash would recrash on
   retry), but it is a real classifier gap: a genuine *swappable* bad-node
   SIGSEGV would produce the same `rc=143` and also never trigger a spare-swap.
   `rc=143` (SIGTERM) is being treated as clean walltime; it should be
   distinguished from an actual walltime-margin stop (which the wrapper knows it
   initiated). Filed as a follow-up.

**Action taken:** the 2048N continuation (8574390, `afterany:8574387`) was
`qhold`'d -- `afterany` fires on failure too, so it would have grabbed 2072
nodes and hit the identical deterministic crash. 512N + 1024N heads left to run.
No `qdel` (holds are reversible; 2048N may become viable if the init-crash class
is ever fixed upstream).

**What a validated run still needs (for whichever bracket survives init):**
first `step:` markers, SophiaG grad_norm bounded at GBS~6144, validator at the
production dp, first checkpoint. Do not mark any bracket "working" until a
cold-cache first step and checkpoint land.
