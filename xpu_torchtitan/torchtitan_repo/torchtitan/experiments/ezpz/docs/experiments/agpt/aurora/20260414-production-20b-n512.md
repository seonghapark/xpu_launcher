# Production Training: agpt_20b on Aurora (512 nodes)

> Sam Foreman
> 2026-04-14

## Overview

First production training attempt for AuroraGPT-20B on Aurora at scale,
using the SophiaG optimizer with LR determined by the
[LR finder experiments](../../lr-finder/).

**Target:** Train agpt_20b on 4.67T tokens of olmo-mix-1124 data.

**Expected at 512 nodes:**
- GBS = 512 * 12 * 1 / 1 = 6144
- Training steps = 4.67T / (6144 * 8192) = 92,859
- At ~346 TPS per 2-node pair, ~88,000 TPS aggregate
- ~53M tokens/step, ~4.67T tokens total
- Estimated time per step: ~0.6s
- Estimated total training time: ~15.5 hours (exceeds 12h walltime)

**Concern:** At 92,859 steps and ~0.6s/step (optimistic), total training time
is ~15.5 hours. The 12-hour walltime will not be enough for a single-shot run.
Checkpointing every 100 steps ensures progress is saved, allowing the job to
resume from the latest checkpoint in a subsequent submission.

## Lessons Learned

1. **torch.compile startup:** ~4 minutes on 2 nodes at 20B scale. At 512 nodes
   this should be similar (compile is per-rank, not coordinated), but
   the mpiexec + env setup phase may take longer.

2. **Data cache race:** The blendcorpus index cache must be built for each
   new (seq_len, GBS) combination. At GBS=6144 this will be a new cache key.
   First run may hit `FileNotFoundError` on non-rank-0 processes.
   See [known-issues.md](../../../guides/known-issues.md#blendcorpus-data-cache-race-condition).

3. **TPS variability:** The 2-node verify showed 10x TPS swings (39-346),
   likely from network contention. At 512 nodes this variability may be worse.
   The steady-state average (~280 TPS per 2-node) is more representative
   than the peak.

4. **Gradient spikes:** Two large grad_norm spikes (1536 and 15.6) occurred
   but training remained stable. SophiaG's second-order Hessian approximation
   may help dampen the effect of these spikes.

5. **Walltime planning:** 92,859 steps at 512 nodes exceeds a single 12h
   allocation. Plan for checkpoint-resume across multiple job submissions.
